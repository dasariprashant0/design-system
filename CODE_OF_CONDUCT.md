# Code of conduct

We want Monoline to be a calm, useful place to work on a small piece of software.

## Our standard

- Be kind and direct. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of gatekeeping.
- Accept that people have different experience, language and ability.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeat it. Report a problem by contacting the maintainer through a private GitHub security advisory or the email on their profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests and any space where someone represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
